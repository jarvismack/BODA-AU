Terms & Privacy

BODA AU terms of service, privacy notice, and data handling information for passenger and driver services in Zanzibar.

Operator and Scope

BODA AU operates a ride request platform for passengers, drivers, and administrators supporting bodaboda and bajaji transport services in Zanzibar.

By using the app or website, you agree to provide accurate account information, use the service lawfully, and follow ride, safety, and payment rules that apply to your role.

Data We Collect

We may collect account details such as full name, phone number, language preference, and optional profile photo when you register or update your profile.

For ride operations, we process pickup and dropoff locations, ride status updates, fare and distance details, ride history, ratings, support requests, and app activity needed to run the service.

For driver accounts, we may also process station details, vehicle details, license number, plate number, verification documents, and availability status.

If you choose to use safety features, we process emergency contact details that you provide. If you enable device location, the app may access approximate or precise location for ride matching, route context, and driver availability updates.

How We Use Data

We use personal data to create and secure accounts, match passengers with drivers, manage ride requests, support customer care, verify drivers, improve safety, detect misuse, and maintain service operations.

We may use your phone number to send operational communications such as registration confirmation, verification updates, ride coordination messages, and safety-related alerts.

We use notification permissions to deliver app alerts when available on your device.

How Data Is Shared

Passenger and driver contact details, profile photos, and ride details may be shared only with the matched ride parties and authorized operations staff when necessary for coordination, support, and safety.

We may share limited information with service providers that support messaging, hosting, analytics, or infrastructure only to the extent needed to operate the service. We do not sell personal data.

Data Retention and Security

We retain account, ride, support, and security records for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, investigate incidents, prevent fraud, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

Security controls include authenticated sessions, access restrictions by role, validation of uploaded files, and request monitoring. No internet or mobile service can be guaranteed fully secure, but we work to protect the data we process.

Your Choices

You may update profile details such as language, phone number, and image within the service where supported. You may decline optional permissions, but some ride, mapping, or notification features may not work correctly without them.

For data correction, account closure, or deletion requests, contact BODA AU support at support@zanzibarbodaboda.tz or through the Help & Contact page. We may keep limited records where required for safety, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

Conduct and Eligibility

Passengers must provide accurate trip details and follow driver safety instructions. Drivers must maintain valid licensing, vehicle information, and truthful verification records. Misuse of the platform may lead to suspension or removal.

Contact and Updates

If you have privacy or service questions, contact BODA AU support by email at support@zanzibarbodaboda.tz, by phone at +255 787 104 836, or through the Help & Contact page.

This notice may be updated as the service changes. Continued use of the service after updates means the revised terms apply.
