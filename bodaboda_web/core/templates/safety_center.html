Safety First

Safety Center

Guidelines and safety controls for passengers, drivers, and platform operations.

Passenger Safety

  • Confirm driver photo, name, and vehicle plate before boarding.
  • Share your trip details with a trusted contact.
  • Use in-app ride flow only. Avoid off-platform payments.

Driver Safety

  • Accept rides only when profile and vehicle details match records.
  • Keep helmet and safety equipment ready for each trip.
  • Report suspicious requests immediately through support channels.

Emergency Process

  • Stop at a safe public place if there is conflict or risk.
  • Contact local authorities for urgent incidents.
  • Log incident details with time, location, and ride ID.

Operational Safety Promise

Every ride is tracked with status transitions, authenticated users, and verified driver workflows to reduce fraud and unsafe operations.
